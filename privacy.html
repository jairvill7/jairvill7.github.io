Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Ranked Loot Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Ranked Loot (@RankedLoot), for that channel only.

What it accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's own videos.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's own videos and statistics.

It does not request permission to delete videos, and it does not access any other account or channel.

How the data is kept

The authorization is stored only on the owner's own computer. Video statistics are kept there to measure the channel's results, until the owner deletes them. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
